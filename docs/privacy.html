WLH Glass Privacy Policy

Applies to the WLH Glass TV app (Google TV, Android TV, Fire TV) and WLH Glass Web. Last updated October 31, 2026.

The short version

WLH Glass has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Wilhoite Lux Horizon LLC, the publisher, does not collect, store or sell any information about you. Your settings and photos stay on your TV or in your browser.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings: favorites, profiles, hidden and locked apps, PIN, weather location, clocks, colors and sports teams.
  • Photos you add for backgrounds and the screensaver.
  • The list of apps you recently opened from WLH Glass.

None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the browser's site data for WLH Glass Web) deletes it.

Purchases

When WLH Glass isn't unlocked yet, the TV (or browser) sends its device ID (a random ID Android or WLH Glass Web makes for this app) to the WLH Glass unlock service so it can check for a key. Payment happens on Stripe's page on your phone; WLH Glass never sees your card. The service stores the device ID, the key, your license code, the email you gave Stripe at checkout, the Stripe checkout ID, the devices the license was moved to, and whether the Downloader codes page has been opened. We use your email only to check a Restore purchase request and to answer support emails. Stripe's privacy policy covers the payment.

Feedback

Settings › Send feedback shows a QR code. Scanning it opens a draft email on your own phone with your TV model, Android version, app version and language filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Accessibility service (optional)

The optional "WLH Glass Home button" uses Android's accessibility service only to notice when you press the remote's Home button, so it can open WLH Glass instead of the built-in home screen, and to run the sleep timer. If you set a screen time limit, it also reads the package name of the app that comes to the front (for example com.netflix.ninja) so it can count minutes per day; those totals stay on the TV. It does not read screen content, text or anything you type, and sends nothing anywhere. You can turn it off any time in WLH Glass › Settings › Home button or in the TV's Accessibility settings.

Photos from your phone

"Add photos from phone" starts a small receiver on your home network only while that screen is open. It requires a one-time code shown on your TV and stops when you leave the screen. Photos go straight from your phone to your TV.

Moving your setup to the web

"Use on the web" puts your settings inside the link (the QR code). Nothing is uploaded; whoever has the link can see those settings, so share it only with your own devices. It never contains your PIN or photos.

Children

WLH Glass doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
